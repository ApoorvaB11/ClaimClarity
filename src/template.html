ClaimClarity

Remittance code translation and denial-pattern reporting
Reporting tool · not an adjudication engine
About this demo. The built-in 600 claims are synthetic: no real members, providers or payers. The denial and adjustment reasons are real X12 codes (CARC and RARC), with wording taken from the published X12 code lists. The grouping into five categories is the author's own and is not an industry standard. This tool translates, groups and reports. It does not decide, recommend or predict anything about a claim's approval, denial, appeal or resubmission.

Data source An 835 is a remittance advice. It is the electronic file a health plan sends a provider after it processes claims. Claim by claim, it lists what was billed, what was paid, and the numeric reason codes behind any adjustment or denial. Files usually end in .835, .edi or .txt. ClaimClarity reads one in your browser and translates the codes. No file handy? Use "Load sample 835 file".

Or paste 835 text

Reading happens in your browser; the page does not upload or store your file. Only if you click "Rewrite with Claude" are the summary figures shown on this page (counts, rates, totals and a payer name) sent to Claude; the claim list is never sent. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Use test or de-identified files only; do not load real patient data.
